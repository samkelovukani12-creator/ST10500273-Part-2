<div style="background-color:#D4AF37; padding:10px; border-radius:8px;"><h1>1. INTRODUCTION</h1></div>

## WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT PROPOSAL

This proposal outlines the design and development of a professional website for Mhlanga Holdings. The website aims to establish a strong digital presence, promote financial inclusion, and provide accessible investment information to South Africans.

The website will support the company’s mission of empowering individuals through affordable investment opportunities and financial education.

---

<div style="background-color:#0B1F3A; color:white; padding:10px; border-radius:8px;"><h1>2. ORGANISATION OVERVIEW</h1></div>

## Company Name

Mhlanga Holdings

## Background

Mhlanga Holdings is a startup financial services company focused on providing accessible, flexible, and inclusive investment opportunities to South Africans.

The company aims to bridge the gap between individuals seeking investment opportunities and the lack of accessible financial platforms.

## Mission Statement

To empower individuals by providing affordable and accessible investment opportunities and financial education.

## Vision Statement

To become a leading and trusted investment platform in South Africa that promotes financial inclusion and long-term financial growth.
