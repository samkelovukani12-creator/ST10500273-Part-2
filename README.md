1. Organisation Overview
Company Name: Mhlanga Holdings
Background:
Mhlanga Holdings is a startup financial services company focused on providing accessible, flexible, and inclusive investment opportunities to South Africans. The company aims to bridge the gap between individuals who want to invest and the lack of accessible financial platforms.
Mission Statement:
To empower individuals by providing affordable and accessible investment opportunities and financial education.
Vision Statement:
To become a leading and trusted investment platform in South Africa that promotes financial inclusion.
Target Audience:
•	Individuals aged 20 years and above 
•	First-time investors 
•	Low- to middle-income earners 
•	People interested in financial growth and education
